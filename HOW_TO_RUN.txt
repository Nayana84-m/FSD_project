------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO RUN LOCALLY (Zero Installation Required!)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Open the project folder:
        D:\FSD_Project\smart-roster-manager\

Step 2: Double-click 'index.html' to open the Master Landing Page in any
        modern browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Firefox, Safari).

Step 3: (Optional Developer Mode)
        If using VS Code, right-click 'index.html' and select 'Open with Live Server'.

2. Employee Availability Portal ('employee.html'):
   - Click "Staff Portal" in the top navigation.
   - Tap any slot in the Monday-to-Sunday timetable grid to smoothly cycle:
     Available  ->  Preferred ⭐  ->  Unavailable ✕  ->  Available
   - Click "💾 Save & Submit Availability" to trigger the live confirmation toast.
   - View confirmed upcoming shifts with weekly hours and earnings in Rs.

3. Store Manager Roster Studio ('manager.html'):
   - Click "Manager Roster" in the top navigation.
   - Tap role filter pills (All Roles, Baristas, Cashiers, Kitchen/Prep) to filter live.
   - Click "Assign Staff" on any open slot to open the real-time pre-check modal.
   - Confirm assignment to instantly update the schedule and labor budget counters.
